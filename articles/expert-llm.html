Do "Expert" Personas Really Enhance LLM Performance?





Experiment

Published: July 15th, 2026

Last updated: July 15th, 2026

---

It has been argued that giving an LLM an expert persona helps the model to tap into its already large knowledge base from pre-training to produce better answers.1 It has also been noted that using an expert persona produces more detailed, longer answers.2 But while expert personas may improve human alignment, do they actually improve the accuracy of answers?

To test this, I conducted an experiment using gemini-2.5-flash-lite (with temperature set to zero to reduce randomness) on 500 randomly selected questions from the MMLU-pro dataset. The LLM answered the same questions under a control condition with no persona and an experimental condition with an expert persona. The results were then analysed via bootstrapping and the Exact McNemar's test.

Despite observing a 1 percentage point increase in accuracy for the expert persona condition, the inferential statistical methods used here suggest that this is likely due to chance. This is supported by more recent research indicating that while expert personas may improve formatting and style, they do not improve (and sometimes even hurt) factual recall and reasoning.3
