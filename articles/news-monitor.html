Using LLMs to decide which news stories to summarise





Project

June 15th, 2026

---

NewsMonitor is a lightweight news monitoring project designed to analyse large volumes of news at a relatively low cost. This is made possible with a batch identification process whereby an LLM is given many different scraped headlines from various news sources and asked to choose which stories should be scraped and summarised based on a certain monitoring objective (e.g. growing political risk in Colombia or emergent tech trends in London etc.).

The process is made more efficient by saving the already processed headlines so that they can be discarded rather than be processed again by the LLM on new runs. This allows the user to do more frequent runs (daily or even hourly) without having to worry about the extra costs associated with processing the same news stories twice.

Once all the news story texts are collected, tens of thousands (or more) of words are split into batches and summarised by a weaker LLM (gemini-2.5-flash). A stronger LLM (gemini-2.5-pro) is then used to create the final summary. This ensures that the summarised content more closely fits the monitoring and formatting specifications in a cost optimised way.
