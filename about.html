🌱 Personal journey

My interest in technical solutions first began during my master's thesis in 2018 while studying "Political Economy of Emerging Markets" at King's College London. I used Tableau to visualise the changing spatial distribution of Chinese innovation over a 8 year period.

After developing an intense fascination with China's rising tech capabilities (I am still amazed by WeChat Pay), I spent a year teaching English in Beijing from 2019 to 2020. I then got stuck in Cambodia for 4 months during the initial wave of Covid-19 while travelling Southeast Asia. Over this time, I began to learn the R programming language for data analysis.

I soon realised that I'd caught the travel bug and proceeded to spend the few several years traveling Europe, the Middle East and Latin America as a freelance academic editor on Upwork. This led to me to edit various academic articles and PhD theses on machine learning, sparking my interest in AI.

I then learnt how rewarding it can be to create real tools people can use while making a Retool database app for an NGO in Turkey in 2022. This newfound sense of accomplishment was reinforced several years later in 2024 when I became the duty manager of a London hostel and implemented various Excel and Python-based productivity tools.

I subsequently began freelance microapp development for Mosaique Creative's corporate intranet clients in 2025, teaching me how to make API calls, configure OAuth 2.0 connectors and parse JSON output. Finally, I combined my newly acquired software skills, passion for building tools and interest in AI to become an AI Application Engineer in 2026.
