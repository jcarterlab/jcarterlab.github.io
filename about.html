💼 What I do

I’m an AI application engineer specialising in using Python to build LLM-powered systems that solve real-world business problems. I can build cost-efficient tools using Gradio and Hugging Face as well as scalable solutions using Next.js and Google Cloud Platform.

🌱 Personal journey

My technical experience began in 2018 when I used Tableau to visualise the changing spatial dynamics of Chinese innovation during a Political Economy of Emerging Markets masters at King's College London.

After developing an interest in China's growing tech capabilities (I am still amazed by WeChat Pay), I spent a year from 2019 to 2020 teaching English in Beijing. I then decided to learn R for data science after becoming stranded in Cambodia for 4 months while travelling before the spread of Covid-19.

I soon realised that I'd caught the travel bug and proceeded to spend the next few years visiting Europe and the Middle East as a freelance academic editor on Upwork. This exposed me to academic research on machine learning and peaked my interest in AI.

Some of my travel photos.

I then set up a low-code database app for a Turkish NGO in 2022. The newfound sense of accomplishment I experienced building real tools that others can use was reinforced again in 2024 when I resumed a duty manager role at a London hostel and created various productivity tools in Excel and Python.

Following this, I began building intranet integrations for Mosaique Creative’s corporate clients on a freelance basis in 2025. This gave me the software skills needed to combine my interest in AI and passion for making tools to become an AI application engineer.

I now build a variety of LLM-powered productivity tools for businesses, including monitoring pipelines, retreival augmented generation (RAG) systems and agentic workflows.
