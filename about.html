💼 What I do

I’m an AI engineer specialising in using Python to build LLM-powered tools that solve business problems. I can build cost-efficient solutions using Gradio and Hugging Face as well as more scalable solutions using Google Cloud Platform. Past projects of mine include agents, retrieval augmented generation (RAG) systems, news monitoring pipelines, LLM evaluations and more.




💻 Tech stack

Python · FastAPI · Next.js · Docker · Google Cloud Platform · GitHub




📍 How I got here

My technical experience began in 2018 during my Political Economy of Emerging Markets masters degree at King's College London. I used Tableau to visualise the changing spatial dynamics of Chinese innovation, finding that the country's inland regions were becoming increasingly innovative.

After developing an interest in China's growing tech capabilities (I am still amazed by WeChat Pay), I spent a year teaching English in Beijing from 2019 to 2020. I then decided to learn R after becoming stranded while travelling in Cambodia in the wake of the Covid-19 pandemic.

I realised that I'd caught the travel bug and spent the next few years visiting Europe and the Middle East while working as an online freelance academic editor. This exposed me to academic research on data science and machine learning, peaking my interest in AI.

I then set up a Retool database app while volunteering for a Turkish NGO in 2022.
