💼 What I do

I’m an AI engineer specialising in using Python to build LLM-powered tools that solve real business problems. I can build cost-efficient solutions using Gradio and Hugging Face as well as more scalable solutions using next.js and Google Cloud Platform. Past projects of mine include agents, retrieval augmented generation (RAG) systems, news monitoring pipelines, LLM evaluations and more.




💻 Technologies

Python · FastAPI · Next.js · Docker · Google Cloud Platform · GitHub




📍 How I got here

My technical experience began in 2018 during my Political Economy of Emerging Markets masters degree at King's College London. I used Tableau to visualise the changing spatial dynamics of Chinese innovation, finding that the country's inland regions were becoming increasingly innovative.

After developing an interest in China's growing tech capabilities (I am still amazed by WeChat Pay), I spent a year teaching English in Beijing from 2019 to 2020. I then decided to learn R after becoming stranded while travelling in Cambodia in the wake of the Covid-19 pandemic.

I soon realised that I'd caught the travel bug and spent the next few years visiting Europe, the Middle East and Latin America while working as an online freelance academic editor. This exposed me to academic research on data science and machine learning, peaking my interest in AI.

Travel photography

Sunset fading over the Nile

While travelling, I set up a Retool database app for an NGO in Turkey and made a Power BI data visualisation dashboard for political risk consultancy in Colombia. After returning to the UK to become the duty manager of a London-based hostel in 2023, I created a chatbot that answered staff questions by efficiently searching the company's sixty page manual using Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).

I then began collaborating with the LumApps implementation team at Mosaique Creative to build intranet integrations for their corporate clients in 2025. This added depth to my technical knowledge and allowed me to further develop my project mangement skills.

I now build LLM-powered tools that help businesses optimise their processes and create value. If you'd like to learn more, you can explore the links below. My blog shares high-level overviews of projects, experiments and insights, while my GitHub showcases the code behind it all.

And of course, don't forget to reach out!
